# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and the people using it.
- Show empathy toward other contributors.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Anything else you'd reasonably expect a maintainer to remove.

## Scope

Applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone
is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **Shaazkhan153@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers may remove comments, close pull requests, or block accounts for
behaviour they judge inappropriate.
